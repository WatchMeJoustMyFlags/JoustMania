# Use Case: Hear Game Audio

## Overview

**Use Case ID:** UC-031
**Use Case Name:** Hear Game Audio
**Primary Actor:** Player
**Goal:** Hear music whose tempo sets the pace, plus sound effects and announcements
**Status:** Implemented
**Requirements:** FR-035, FR-036, FR-037, FR-038

## Preconditions

- The audio service runs with `play_audio` on and an ALSA device (USB audio on a Pi 5).

## Main Success Scenario

1. System plays lobby music at `audio_volume.lobby_music` while the menu waits.
2. At game start, System stops lobby music and plays game music (`Joust/music/*.ogg`) at `audio_volume.countdown_music` during the countdown, then at `audio_volume.game`.
3. System switches the music between normal speed (1.0×) and 1.3× after random windows (default 1.0× windows 10 to 23 s, 1.3× windows 4 to 8 s; towards the end 1.0× windows shorten to 8 to 12 s while 1.3× windows become 6 to 10 s), with a 1.5 s audio transition; thresholds follow the tempo (UC-030).
4. System plays effects and voice lines on events: beeps and a start sound at the countdown, an explosion on death, "congratulations" for the winner at the end, and the agent's sounds: the audio cue `agent_cue` (`play_audio_cue`) and the shield sound `shield_up` (UC-040 BR-007). Voice lines of the other modes ("<colour> team wins", werewolf reveal, zombie timer) are mode-specific and documented in UC-021, UC-022 and UC-023.
5. System mixes up to 8 effect channels over the music. The audio service has one master volume for music and effects: the `audio_volume.*` flags set it in turn, so effects play at the volume of the current phase.

## Alternative Flows

### A1: Audio muted

**Trigger:** `play_audio` is off (step 1)
**Flow:**

1. System accepts all requests and plays nothing (`PlaySound` and `PlayMusic` spans carry `audio.muted`); `play_audio` is read at each `PlaySound` / `PlayMusic` and on flag changes. Turning it off at runtime stops the music; turning it on again resumes audio, also when the service started muted: the audio hardware is opened then, off the event loop. If the open fails the service stays muted and retries at the next read (#1352).
2. Use case ends.

### A2: All channels busy

**Trigger:** 8 effects are playing (step 5)
**Flow:**

1. A CRITICAL request steals a lower-priority channel; any other request is dropped and counted (`audio_channel_saturation_total`). No caller sends CRITICAL today.
2. Use case continues at step 5.

### A3: Agent changes tempo or volume

**Trigger:** The agent applies `adjust_music_tempo`, `ramp_tempo`, `set_pacing_profile` or `adjust_volume` (UC-040) (step 3)
**Flow:**

1. System overrides the tempo schedule or volume for the rest of the game (or until cleared); when the override clears, the schedule resumes from the current state.
2. Use case continues at step 3.

### A4: Voice change

**Trigger:** Operator changes `menu_voice` (aaron / ivy) (step 4)
**Flow:**

1. The audio service picks up the change on the next flag change event.
2. Use case continues at step 4.

### A5: Agent-driven tempo schedule

**Trigger:** `tempo_schedule_mode` is `agent` instead of the default `rule` (step 3)
**Flow:**

1. System takes the delay until the next tempo change from an agent tempo directive; with no directive it uses the default random windows. The flag is re-read live during the game.
2. Use case continues at step 3.

## Postconditions

### Success Postconditions

- Music and effects match the game state.

### Failure Postconditions

- Audio failures never stop a game. Playback errors are counted in `audio_playback_errors_total`, a missing file or a full mixer in `audio_sounds_played_total{result=file_not_found|no_channel}` and `audio_channel_saturation_total`; coordinator-side RPC failures are only logged.

## Business Rules

### BR-001: Tempo modes

Free-for-All drives the tempo loop of step 3. Modes that run their own game loop without tempo changes are mode-specific and documented in their use cases (Werewolf UC-022 BR-001, Zombies UC-023 BR-002, Tournament and Fight Club UC-025 BR-002; moved there by #1500).

### BR-002: Intervention cue is visual

`agent_intervention_cue` controls a magenta LED pulse for hard interventions, not a sound.
