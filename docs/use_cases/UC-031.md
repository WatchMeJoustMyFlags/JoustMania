# Use Case: Hear Game Audio

## Overview

**Use Case ID:** UC-031
**Use Case Name:** Hear Game Audio
**Primary Actor:** Player
**Goal:** Hear music whose tempo sets the pace, plus sound effects and announcements
**Status:** Implemented
**Requirements:** FR-035, FR-036, FR-037, FR-038

## Preconditions

- The audio service runs with `play_audio` on and an ALSA device (USB audio on a Pi 5).

## Main Success Scenario

1. System plays lobby music at `audio_volume.lobby_music` while the menu waits.
2. At game start, System stops lobby music and plays game music (`Joust/music/*.ogg`) at `audio_volume.countdown_music` during the countdown, then at `audio_volume.game`.
3. System switches the music between slow (1.0×) and fast (1.3×) after random windows (default slow 10 to 23 s, fast 4 to 8 s; towards the end slow windows shorten to 8 to 12 s while fast windows become 6 to 10 s), with a 1.5 s audio transition; thresholds follow the tempo (UC-030).
4. System plays effects and voice lines on events: beeps and a start sound at the countdown, an explosion on death, "congratulations" or "<colour> team wins" at the end, mode-specific lines (werewolf reveal, zombie timer).
5. System mixes up to 8 effect channels over the music.

## Alternative Flows

### A1: Audio muted

**Trigger:** `play_audio` is off
**Flow:**

1. System accepts all requests and plays nothing (spans carry `audio.muted`); turning it off at runtime stops the music.

### A2: All channels busy

**Trigger:** 8 effects are playing (step 5)
**Flow:**

1. A CRITICAL request steals a lower-priority channel; any other request is dropped. No caller sends CRITICAL today.

### A3: Agent changes tempo or volume

**Trigger:** The agent applies `adjust_music_tempo`, `ramp_tempo`, `set_pacing_profile` or `adjust_volume` (UC-040)
**Flow:**

1. System overrides the tempo schedule or volume for the rest of the game (or until cleared).

### A4: Voice change

**Trigger:** Operator changes `menu_voice` (aaron / ivy)
**Flow:**

1. The audio service picks up the change on the next flag change event.

## Postconditions

### Success Postconditions

- Music and effects match the game state.

### Failure Postconditions

- Audio failures never stop a game; errors are counted (`audio_playback_errors_total`).

## Business Rules

### BR-001: Tempo modes

Werewolf, Zombies, Tournament and Fight Club do not change tempo (D-G3).

### BR-002: Intervention cue is visual

`agent_intervention_cue` controls a magenta LED pulse for hard interventions, not a sound.
