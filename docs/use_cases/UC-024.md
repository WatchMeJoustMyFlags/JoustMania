# Use Case: Play Swapper

## Overview

**Use Case ID:** UC-024
**Use Case Name:** Play Swapper
**Primary Actor:** Player
**Goal:** Be on the team everyone ends up on, without being the last one swapped
**Status:** Implemented
**Requirements:** FR-024, FR-142

## Preconditions

- A Swapper game was started with at least two players (UC-010); always two teams.

## Main Success Scenario

1. System splits players into two teams.
2. A player whose movement exceeds the threshold switches to the other team (`player_swapped`) and gets a grace period of `death_grace_period_seconds`.
3. When every player is on one team, System publishes `swapper_winner` (team, winners, excluded serial): the last swapped player does not win.
4. System publishes `game_ended`.

## Alternative Flows

### A1: Disconnect

**Trigger:** A player disconnects (step 2)
**Flow:**

1. System eliminates the player; win detection continues with the remaining players.
2. Use case continues at step 3.

## Postconditions

### Success Postconditions

- The winners are in the event stream; the session is ENDED.

### Failure Postconditions

- `game_error` ends the session.

## Business Rules

### BR-001: Last swap excluded

The player whose death completed the swap is excluded from the winners.

Known gap (preconditions): `lib/types.py` declares a minimum of 3 players for Swapper, but only the two-player minimum of UC-010 BR-001 is enforced (D-G2, issue #1277).

Known gap (A1): a disconnect runs the swap instead of an elimination, so the disconnected controller stays a team member, counts for convergence and can win (issue #1344).

Known gap (step 2): the base death effect runs after the swap, so a swapped player's LED ends black instead of the new team colour (issue #1342).

Known gap (end of game): a force end still declares a winner next to `game_force_ended`: the end-game step publishes the winner, the rainbow, the win voice and `game_ended` (issue #1343, D-G16; moved from UC-027 BR-001 by #1602).

**Code / tests:** `services/game_coordinator/games/swapper.py` (`_kill_player_impl`, `_get_alive_teams`); tests `tests/integration/test_parallel_lifecycle.py` (Swapper).
