# Use Case: Play Swapper

## Overview

**Use Case ID:** UC-024
**Use Case Name:** Play Swapper
**Primary Actor:** Player
**Goal:** Be on the team everyone ends up on, without being the last one swapped
**Status:** Tested
**Requirements:** FR-024, FR-170

## Preconditions

- A Swapper game was started with at least three players, the mode's minimum (UC-010 BR-001); always two teams.

## Main Success Scenario

1. System splits players into two teams.
2. A player whose movement exceeds the threshold switches to the other team (`player_swapped`) and gets a grace period of `death_grace_period_seconds`. The controller LED shows one FLASH, then the new team colour; the base death effect is not sent (#1342), and the player stays alive in the `player_alive` gauge and the alive aggregate.
3. When every player is on one team, System publishes `swapper_winner` (team, winners, excluded serial): the last swapped player does not win.
4. System publishes `game_ended`.

## Alternative Flows

### A1: Disconnect

**Trigger:** A player's controller disconnects during the game (step 2), including warm-up
**Flow:**

1. System eliminates the player (#1344): the player is not swapped, stays out of the game and gets the base death effect; its `player_lifecycle` span ends with `player_eliminated` (`reason` = `controller_disconnect`).
2. The player no longer counts for either team: team counts, convergence and the winners leave it out (it stays in the session's player list).
3. A disconnect that arrives while the player's own swap is still running (an `eliminate_player` swap, BR-002) also eliminates it: the swap does not finish, so no new team colour, no `player_swapped` and no swap sound.
4. System runs the win check at once. When only one team is left, the use case continues at step 3; when no player is left, nobody wins.
5. Otherwise the game continues at step 2.

### A2: Instructions on

**Trigger:** The host has switched spoken instructions on (`game_instructions`, FR-014, UC-012 A3) when the game starts (step 1)
**Flow:**

1. System plays the `Swapper-instructions` clip as in UC-020 A5 and waits for it before the music and the countdown. A force-end during the wait skips the countdown and the start, as in UC-020 A5 step 2.
2. Use case continues at step 2.

## Postconditions

### Success Postconditions

- The winners are in the event stream; the session is ENDED.

### Failure Postconditions

- `game_error` ends the session.

## Business Rules

### BR-001: Last swap excluded

The player whose death completed the swap is excluded from the winners. An eliminated player (A1) is never the excluded one. An elimination clears the last swap only when it completes the convergence itself: then nobody is excluded, so the winners list is never empty (D2). When the teams had already converged by the last swap (an `eliminate_player` swap whose win check has not run yet), the last swapped player stays excluded, unless it is the only player left on its team.

### BR-002: Interventions

The agent's `eliminate_player` swaps the player like a motion death (step 2, `death.reason` = `agent_intervention`); it does not remove the player (D1). `revive_player` is denied for Swapper by the intervention capability matrix (`interventions.py`), so an eliminated player (A1) never comes back.

### BR-003: Outcome

Swapper uses the base `run()`, so the game root span records the outcome as in UC-020 BR-003: `completed` for a natural end, or `force_ended` with the force-end reason (`game.end_reason`) when force-ended. A force-end after the loop ended does not rewrite the outcome.

A force-end declares no winner (issue #1343): while the game runs, no `swapper_winner`, no winner rainbow, no grey on the last swapped player, no team victory voice and no rainbow wait happen, the player and team spans end with `winner=false`, and `game_ended` carries `winning_team` `none` with `outcome` `force_ended` and `reason`. A gameplay stream that ends without a win also ends with `winning_team` `none`. A swap that is still pausing when the game ends or the win is latched (BR-004) does not finish: no team colour, no `player_swapped`, no swap sound, no death effect over the end effects. A swap whose new team colour is being written when the game ends or the win is latched keeps the colour and the new team, but publishes no `player_swapped` and plays no swap sound (issue #1988).

### BR-004: Win latch

The win is latched once, in the step that decides it, before `swapper_winner` is published: the winning team, the winners and the excluded player are frozen, and the end-of-game step uses only that snapshot. The game outcome (`completed`) is latched in that same step, so a force-end that lands while the event is being published keeps the winner and `game_ended` names the same team; a force-end that lands before the win check declares no winner (BR-003) (issue #1988). A force-end during the 1 s hold after the win keeps the winner and its end-of-game effects. Once latched, the win check publishes nothing again, and an `eliminate_player` kill no longer swaps or eliminates anyone: the kill gate declines the kill before any death bookkeeping (UC-030 step 9), and the `eliminate_player` is recorded as blocked `eliminate_refused` (UC-040 A22, #1383). A kill already in flight when the win is latched or the game is force-ended is declined by the gate's second check, right before the bookkeeping: no death is counted (its explosion may already have played), and only a player whose disconnect was marked before the latch is still eliminated (A1) (issue #1988). A disconnect is ignored earlier, by `_handle_disconnect`, so the gate never sees it. When every remaining player was eliminated (A1), the latch holds no winner.
