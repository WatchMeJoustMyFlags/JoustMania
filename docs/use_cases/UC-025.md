# Use Case: Play a 1v1 Format

## Overview

**Use Case ID:** UC-025
**Use Case Name:** Play a 1v1 Format (Tournament, Fight Club)
**Primary Actor:** Player
**Goal:** Win duels: become tournament champion or the Fight Club leader
**Status:** Tested
**Requirements:** FR-025, FR-144, FR-171

## Preconditions

- A Tournament or Fight Club game was started with at least the mode's minimum players: three for Tournament, two for Fight Club (UC-010 BR-001). Both modes refuse a start with fewer than two players before they open the gameplay stream: `run()` raises and the session publishes `game_error` (#1273).

## Main Success Scenario

1. For Tournament, System publishes `game_starting`, builds a single-elimination bracket padded to a power of two with byes, opens the gameplay stream and plays `tournament_intro`: every player shows the waiting colour for 3 s while the stream is drained, so intro motion never reaches detection (a stream that broke during the intro is opened once more). System then runs the countdown, publishes `game_started`, primes the EMA filters (UC-030 BR-003) and from then on reads the gameplay stream for the whole game on the base game loop, matches and pauses included (D-G3, D-G33, #1273, #1882). A player with a bye is placed in round 2 when the bracket is built (no match and no pause), so a bracket of any size from 3 players up reaches a champion. Byes are spread over the first round (bit-reversed match order) so each round-2 match gets at most one bye player where the size allows it: two bye players meet in round 2 only when there are more byes than round-2 matches (5 players: 1 such match, 9 players: 3; 6 and 12 players none) (#1994).
2. For each match System publishes `match_start` and lights the two fighters red and blue; both are invincible for `invincibility_seconds` (default 4 s), from the moment they are seated, before `match_start` is published, so a kill or `eliminate_player` during the match start is declined (#2025). The first match of a new round follows `round_start` and a 2 s pause. A gameplay stream reopened during the game (UC-030 BR-006) restores every player's current colour: red or blue for a fighter and for a match winner waiting for its next match (it keeps its match colour), the waiting colour for a player who has not fought yet, and off for an eliminated player.
3. The first player to exceed the threshold loses the match (`match_end`); waiting players watch: their motion is not checked for hits, while their controller name, battery and health are still read (#1737). Only a fighter of the match being played can lose, and only while that match is undecided and the fighter is out of its grace (BR-006, D-G8). A match has one loser (BR-004).
4. After `tournament.time_between_matches_seconds` (default 5 s) the next match starts. The pauses and the match time limit are deadlines checked on each gameplay frame, so the stream is read throughout and the bracket moves on with the frames (D-G33).
5. The step that decides the final (the kill, the match time limit of A1, or a walkover of A4) crowns the champion and latches the result (BR-005). The final gets no match rainbow; its loser's LED goes off and System publishes the final's `match_end` when one is published (a walkover final of A4 has none, and a force-end after the decision skips it), then `tournament_champion` once, ends the game, shows the winner rainbow on the champion, plays the congratulations voice, waits for the rainbow and publishes `player_analytics` for each player (when `analytics.enabled` is on; UC-041 BR-006) and `game_ended` with the champion's serial as `champion` (D-G12, #1337). A bracket that finishes with nobody left has no champion: A3.

## Alternative Flows

### A1: Match timeout

**Trigger:** Nobody loses within `tournament.match_seconds` (default 22 s) (step 3)
**Flow:**

1. System picks a random winner.
2. The loser is counted out like a player who died: `dead_count`, `game_player_alive` 0, its elimination order and the `game_players_alive` aggregate, but with no death: no explosion, no death effect, no `game_player_deaths_total` increment and no `player_death` span event (its LED goes off with step 4).
3. Use case continues at step 4.

### A2: Fight Club

**Trigger:** Mode is FightClub (step 1)
**Flow:**

1. System publishes `game_starting`, opens the gameplay stream and shows every player the waiting colour during the 2 s intro (the stream is drained meanwhile, so intro motion never reaches detection; a stream that broke during the intro is opened once more), runs the countdown, publishes `game_started`, primes the EMA filters (UC-030 BR-003) and then starts the first round: the defender (red) faces a challenger (green); others wait dimmed (D-G3, #1273).
2. Each round lasts `fight_club.round_seconds` (default 22 s) with 4 s invincibility. Only the defender or the fighter of the round being fought, out of its invincibility, can lose it (BR-006); a kill that entered during an earlier round never decides a later one (BR-006, #2015). The winner stays as defender and scores. The loser gets the death effect (`GAME_EFFECT_PLAYER_DEATH`) at once, goes to the back of the queue and stays in the game (`game_player_alive` back to 1); when the game goes on, after a 1 s pause the next round sets every LED, so the loser ends on its waiting colour, or green when it fights next (D-G32); a round that ends the game leads into the end of the game instead, and a force end during the pause starts no next round (no colours, no `round_start`, no round timer). On timeout both go to the back of the queue, and the next round is seated (or the game ends, BR-005) also when the colour writes fail on a gameplay stream that has died: they are skipped, and the reopened stream restores each player's colour (UC-030 BR-006, #2021).
3. After `fight_club.min_rounds` (default 10) rounds, the game ends if one player leads. If the top two scores are tied, the next round is a face-off between the two tied leaders (the first two connected ones in join order when more are tied; when fewer than two of them are connected, A4 step 6): one round with no time limit (`round_start` with `face_off: true`). A defender who is not one of them goes to the front of the queue; nobody is dropped from the rotation and nobody fights itself. The face-off winner leads by one point, so the game ends after it. The face-off stays untimed (original behaviour, by maintainer decision): a face-off nobody loses ends only by a force end (UC-027) or by a disconnect (A4: the player who leaves forfeits the face-off).
4. System shows the winner rainbow on the leader latched when the game decided to end, plays the congratulations voice, waits for the rainbow and publishes `player_analytics` for each player (when `analytics.enabled` is on; UC-041 BR-006) and `game_ended` with the leader as `winner` (BR-005). Without a latched leader (a force end or a stream end before the game decided) it shows no rainbow, plays no voice and publishes `game_ended` with `winner` `none` (BR-003).
5. Use case ends.

### A3: No winner

**Trigger:** The Tournament bracket finishes with nobody left (step 5): every remaining player is out of it, for example all disconnected (A4) and the walkovers leave no one. A force-end is not a trigger (maintainer decision M2, 2026-10-23).
**Flow:**

1. System latches the outcome without a champion in the same synchronous step that finds the bracket empty (BR-003, BR-005), and publishes `tournament_no_winner` once, only while the game is running and before the latch: after a force-end nothing is published.
2. System ends the game without a rainbow and without the congratulations voice, and publishes `game_ended` with `champion` `none`, `outcome` `completed`.
3. Use case ends. A force-end, or a stream end before the bracket finished, gets only `game_ended` with `champion` `none`, its `outcome` and, for a force-end, its `reason`.

### A4: Player disconnects

**Trigger:** A player's controller disconnects during a Tournament game (step 2) or a Fight Club game (A2 steps 1–2)
**Flow:**

1. Tournament: a fighter of the match being played loses it, also during its invincibility: the opponent wins the match (`match_end`) and advances. The disconnect goes through the death path (UC-030 step 9, UC-020 A2).
2. Tournament: a waiting player, or the winner of a match decided in the same frame, is out of the bracket (`alive` false, eliminated, through the same death path). The other player of its next match advances without playing (a walkover: no `match_start` and no `match_end`), at once when that player is known, otherwise when it wins its own match. When both players of a match not yet played are out, the match has no winner and the player of the next match it feeds gets the walkover.
3. Tournament: the bracket goes on with its next match (step 4, D-G33).
4. Fight Club: the defender or the fighter of the round being fought forfeits it, also during its invincibility: the opponent wins the round and scores (`round_end`, as in A2 step 2). The player who left is out for good (`alive` false, never seated again) and keeps its score and rank (`final_scores`, the `final_rank` of its player span). The disconnect goes through the death path (UC-030 step 9, UC-020 A2); the leaver gets no `game_player_alive` 1 again. A round is announced (`round_start`) as soon as it is seated, before its colours and beep: a forfeit during that round start, also while the round timer seats the round after a timeout, comes after its `round_start`, and the game goes on with the next round and its timer (#2025).
5. Fight Club: a waiting player, or the round winner during the 1 s pause between rounds, leaves the rotation the same way (through the death path), and no round is decided. A player whose own kill is still in flight (awaiting its explosion) when it disconnects gets exactly one death: that kill takes it out, as a forfeit when it still fights the round, otherwise out of the rotation. A round loser that disconnects while its lost round is still being finished (the pause) leaves at once, without a second death; when the next round has already seated it again (two players, or a face-off), it forfeits that round (step 4) once the round loss is finished. Until then that round cannot be decided otherwise: a kill of its opponent is declined (BR-006), and when the opponent disconnects too, its disconnect is handled only after the forfeit (the opponent first wins that round and scores, then leaves as the round winner, ignored once the winner is latched, step 6), so the leaver never wins the round it forfeits (#2015). A disconnect during the intro, the countdown or the EMA warm-up is handled before round 1 is seated: the player leaves the rotation (through the death path, score 0), no round is decided, nobody scores, no death effect is sent and there is no round pause; when fewer than two connected players remain, the game ends before round 1 (step 6: every score is 0, so no winner).
6. Fight Club: when fewer than two connected players remain, the game ends at once, whatever the round count, and the leader is latched (BR-005): the one top score wins, also when that player has left (a disconnected leader is crowned: `game_ended.winner` names it, and its winner rainbow goes to a controller that is gone); a tied top has no winner (`winner` `none`, outcome `completed`). After `fight_club.min_rounds`, a tied top where fewer than two of the tied leaders are connected also ends the game without a winner (no face-off is possible). A disconnect after the winner is latched is ignored (BR-005; D-G35, #1383).
7. Use case continues at step 4 (Tournament; a Fight Club game that goes on continues at A2 step 2).

### A5: Instructions on

**Trigger:** The host has switched spoken instructions on (`game_instructions`, FR-014, UC-012 A3) when the game starts (step 1, A2 step 1)
**Flow:**

1. After the intro, System plays the mode's clip as in UC-020 A5 and waits for it before the countdown and the music; the gameplay stream is open and drained meanwhile, as during the intro. Tournament plays `Tournament-instructions` in the `menu_voice` voice. Fight Club plays `Fightclub-instructions` in the `menu_voice` voice too: every voice profile has the line (UC-031 BR-007), so no voice is silent (#2151, #2164, #2107). A force-end during the wait skips the countdown and the start (no music, no `game_started`, no `countdown_start`); the gameplay stream is already open in this mode, so it is not skipped but released when the game ends. A force-end during the intro skips the clip.
2. Use case continues at step 2 (Fight Club: A2 step 2).

## Postconditions

### Success Postconditions

- The champion or leader is in the event stream; the session is ENDED.

### Failure Postconditions

- `game_error` ends the session.

## Business Rules

### BR-001: Invincibility composes with agent shields

A player cannot die while `now < max(agent shield, match invincibility)` (#817).

### BR-002: Fixed tempo

Both modes play without tempo changes (D-G3; moved from UC-031 BR-001 by #1500): no tempo loop by design, the music plays at 1.0 and the slow threshold table applies all game. Both run the base game loop and the EMA warm-up (Tournament since #1882). Fight Club and Tournament detect hits for their two fighters with UC-030 steps 4–7 and A1 (Tournament: the two fighters of the current match): the `perception.ema_weight` EMA, the EMA reset during grace and invincibility, and at most one warning per 0.5 s window. Waiting players are not checked (no warning, no death), but their name, battery and controller health are still captured (D-G9, #1347; Tournament #1737).

Fight Club and Tournament start like the base `run()`: they set the game's flag evaluation context before any task starts, run the EMA warm-up of the startup guard (UC-030 BR-003) after the countdown and before the first round or match, and end through the shared run-end cleanup (shield feedback ended before the winner effects on the teardown path; the gameplay stream closed, shield charge spans closed and the session analytics cleared on every exit; D-G29, #1938). Tournament also gets the 0.3 s start grace of the base game loop, on top of the per-match invincibility. An error in Tournament's bracket step before the champion is latched ends the game with `game_error` (no `completed` outcome) instead of a stream reconnect; after the latch the result stands (BR-005). Tests: `services/game_coordinator/tests/test_fight_club_run.py`, `services/game_coordinator/tests/test_tournament_run.py`.

### BR-003: Root-span outcome

Fight Club and Tournament each have their own `run()` (start, intro and teardown) but play on the base game loop (#1996, #2000), and record the game root-span outcome as in UC-020 BR-003: `completed` for a natural end, or `force_ended` with the force-end reason (`game.end_reason`) when force-ended. A force-end after the loop ended does not rewrite the outcome.

A Fight Club force-end declares no winner (issue #1343, D-G16; moved from UC-027 BR-001 by #1602): no winner rainbow, no congratulations voice and no rainbow wait; every player span ends with `winner=false`, and `game_ended` carries `winner` `none` (`winner_score` 0) with `outcome` `force_ended` and `reason`. A gameplay stream that ends before the game decided also ends with `winner` `none`. A tie ends the game only through A4 step 6 (fewer than two connected players, or fewer than two connected tied leaders after `fight_club.min_rounds`), with no winner (`_leader()` returns none on a tied top); otherwise a face-off is seated (step 3). A kill of the round winner during the 1 s pause between rounds is declined at the kill gate (no round is fought, BR-006).

A Tournament force-end before the final is decided crowns no champion (#1337, #1343): no `tournament_champion`, no winner rainbow, no congratulations voice and no rainbow wait; every player span ends with `is_champion=false`, and `game_ended` carries `champion` `none` with `outcome` `force_ended` and `reason`. A gameplay stream that ends before the final is decided also ends with `champion` `none` (`outcome` `completed`).

### BR-004: One loser per match

In Tournament, the first kill of a match decides it. A second lethal sample in the same frame (a double hit), once the match is decided, does not eliminate the winner: the winner stays in the bracket and advances (step 4), and only the loser gets `GAME_EFFECT_PLAYER_DEATH`. A kill the mode ignores (a waiting player, a player in grace, a decided match) gets no death effect (D-G30, #1991).

Such a kill is declined at the kill gate, before any death accounting: the double-hit winner keeps `game_player_alive` 1, and `dead_count`, the elimination order and `game_player_deaths_total` do not move (BR-006, #1383).

### BR-005: Win latch

Fight Club latches its winner the moment it decides the game is over, on the kill path or the round-timer path, synchronously with the decision and before any await: the leader (the one top score; a tied top latches no winner, which happens only through A4 step 6) and the root-span outcome `completed` (UC-020 BR-003). The end of the game uses only that latch. A force-end that lands after the decision, in the same tick or during the rainbow wait (teardown), keeps the winner; a force-end that lands before the decision latches no winner (BR-003). After the decision no next round starts (#1343).

Tournament latches its champion in the step that decides the final, the kill, the match time limit (A1) or a walkover (A4), synchronously with the decision and before any await: the champion and the root-span outcome `completed` (UC-020 BR-003). The end of the game uses only that latch. A force-end that lands after the decision, in the same tick or during the rainbow wait (teardown), keeps the champion; a force-end that lands before the decision latches none (BR-003), and a kill still awaiting its explosion then is declined at the gate's second check, so it decides no match (UC-030 step 9, #2033); the force-end never lands between that check and the decision (UC-027 step 3, #2149). After the decision the result does not change (a later disconnect of the champion is ignored), and `tournament_champion` is published once. A gameplay stream that the controller manager ends as the final is decided keeps the champion: the final's writes and the end-of-game effects are skipped on the finished call, never `game_error` (UC-030 BR-006, #1986). An error after the champion is latched, in the frame that decided the final (a gRPC error of the gameplay call, such as a failed write of the final loser's death effect, included: no stream reconnect) or while the decided final is finished and the champion announced (`_finish_decided_final`, in that frame or in the end step), is logged and the result stands: the game ends through the teardown with the champion and outcome `completed`, never `game_error` (D-G34). The end step's own `game_ended` publish is not guarded: if it fails, the game ends with `game_error` like every mode (UC-020 BR-003). `tournament_champion` carries `rounds_played`, the round of the final, also for a final won by a walkover.

### BR-006: Interventions

Tournament has a kill gate (`_accepts_kill`, UC-030 step 9): it takes a kill only for a fighter of the match being played while that match is undecided and the fighter is out of its grace (match invincibility or agent shield, BR-001), or for a player whose controller disconnected (A4), and no kill at all once the champion is latched (BR-005). Every other kill is declined before any death accounting: no explosion (unless the gate closed while the kill path awaited, UC-030 step 9), no `dead_count`, no `game_player_alive` 0, no elimination order, no `game_player_deaths_total`, no `player_death` span event and no death effect (#1383, D-G8).

- `eliminate_player` on a fighter of the undecided match out of its grace makes it lose the match: the opponent wins and advances (`match_end`), and on the final the opponent is the champion (step 5).
- `eliminate_player` on a waiting player, a player with a bye, a fighter in grace, between matches (no match being played, or the match already decided) or after the champion is latched is refused: `eliminate_refused` (UC-040 A22), nothing applied.
- `revive_player` is denied by the capability matrix (`mode_unsupported`, UC-040 A22): a revive would break the bracket.

Fight Club has a kill gate (`_accepts_kill`, UC-030 step 9): it takes a kill only for the defender or the fighter of the round being fought while it is out of its grace (round invincibility or agent shield, BR-001), or for a player whose controller disconnected while that player is still in the game (A4; once it is out, a second kill of it, such as a disconnect kill still in flight, is declined, so it is never counted twice), and no kill at all once the winner is latched (BR-005). Between a round's decision (the kill, or the timeout of A2 step 2, which closes the round before its first await) and the seating of the next pair no round is fought. Each seated round has its own id: a kill that entered the gate during one round and still awaits (its explosion) when another round is seated is declined at the second check, also when the same player fights the new round out of its grace (`invincibility_seconds` 0); a disconnected player's kill still counts (A4). While a seated combatant's disconnect waits for its round-loss kill to finish (A4 step 5), the round accepts no kill of the other combatant, not even its disconnect kill: a disconnect of the other combatant is handled after the forfeit (#2015). Every other kill is declined before any death accounting: no explosion (unless the gate closed while the kill path awaited, UC-030 step 9), no `dead_count`, no `game_player_alive` 0, no elimination order, no `game_player_deaths_total`, no `player_death` span event and no death effect (#1383, D-G35).

- `eliminate_player` on the defender or the fighter of the round being fought, out of its grace, makes it lose the round: the opponent wins and scores (`round_end`, A2 step 2).
- `eliminate_player` on a waiting player, on a combatant during its invincibility, between rounds (the round winner during the 1 s pause, or during a timeout) or after the winner is latched is refused: `eliminate_refused` (UC-040 A22), nothing applied.
- `revive_player` is denied by the capability matrix (`mode_unsupported`, UC-040 A22): a revive would break the queue.
