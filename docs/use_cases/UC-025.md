# Use Case: Play a 1v1 Format

## Overview

**Use Case ID:** UC-025
**Use Case Name:** Play a 1v1 Format (Tournament, Fight Club)
**Primary Actor:** Player
**Goal:** Win duels: become tournament champion or the Fight Club leader
**Status:** Implemented
**Requirements:** FR-025, FR-144, FR-171

## Preconditions

- A Tournament or Fight Club game was started with at least two players (UC-010). Both modes refuse a start with fewer than two players before they open the gameplay stream: `run()` raises and the session publishes `game_error` (#1273).

## Main Success Scenario

1. For Tournament, System publishes `game_starting`, builds a single-elimination bracket padded to a power of two with byes, opens the gameplay stream and plays `tournament_intro`: every player shows the waiting colour for 3 s while the stream is drained, so intro motion never reaches detection (a stream that broke during the intro is opened once more). System then runs the countdown, publishes `game_started`, primes the EMA filters (UC-030 BR-003) and from then on reads the gameplay stream for the whole game on the base game loop, matches and pauses included (D-G3, D-G33, #1273, #1882). A player with a bye is placed in round 2 when the bracket is built (no match and no pause), so a bracket of any size from 3 players up reaches a champion.
2. For each match System publishes `match_start` and lights the two fighters red and blue; both are invincible for `invincibility_seconds` (default 4 s). The first match of a new round follows `round_start` and a 2 s pause. A gameplay stream reopened during the game (UC-030 BR-006) restores every player's current colour: the fighters' red and blue, the waiting colour, and off for an eliminated player.
3. The first player to exceed the threshold loses the match (`match_end`); waiting players watch: their motion is not checked for hits, while their controller name, battery and health are still read (#1737; an `eliminate_player` intervention on a waiting player still reaches the death accounting, D-G8, #1383). A match has one loser (BR-004).
4. After `tournament.time_between_matches_seconds` (default 5 s) the next match starts. The pauses and the match time limit are deadlines checked on each gameplay frame, so the stream is read throughout and the bracket moves on with the frames (D-G33).
5. When one player remains, System publishes `tournament_champion` and `game_ended`.

## Alternative Flows

### A1: Match timeout

**Trigger:** Nobody loses within `tournament.match_seconds` (default 22 s) (step 3)
**Flow:**

1. System picks a random winner.
2. Use case continues at step 4.

### A2: Fight Club

**Trigger:** Mode is FightClub (step 1)
**Flow:**

1. System publishes `game_starting`, opens the gameplay stream and shows every player the waiting colour during the 2 s intro (the stream is drained meanwhile, so intro motion never reaches detection; a stream that broke during the intro is opened once more), runs the countdown, publishes `game_started`, primes the EMA filters (UC-030 BR-003) and then starts the first round: the defender (red) faces a challenger (green); others wait dimmed (D-G3, #1273).
2. Each round lasts `fight_club.round_seconds` (default 22 s) with 4 s invincibility. The winner stays as defender and scores. The loser gets the death effect (`GAME_EFFECT_PLAYER_DEATH`) at once, goes to the back of the queue and stays in the game (`game_player_alive` back to 1); when the game goes on, after a 1 s pause the next round sets every LED, so the loser ends on its waiting colour, or green when it fights next (D-G32); a round that ends the game leads into the end of the game instead. On timeout both go to the back of the queue.
3. After `fight_club.min_rounds` (default 10) rounds, the game ends if one player leads. If the top two scores are tied, the next round is a face-off between the two tied leaders (the first two in join order when more are tied): one round with no time limit (`round_start` with `face_off: true`). A defender who is not one of them goes to the front of the queue; nobody is dropped from the rotation and nobody fights itself. The face-off winner leads by one point, so the game ends after it. A force end (UC-027) is the only way out of a face-off nobody loses.
4. System publishes `game_ended`.
5. Use case ends.

### A3: No winner

**Trigger:** No champion can be determined (step 5)
**Flow:**

1. System publishes `tournament_no_winner`.
2. Use case ends.

### A4: Player disconnects

**Trigger:** A player's controller disconnects during a Tournament game (step 2)
**Flow:**

1. A fighter of the match being played loses it, also during its invincibility: the opponent wins the match (`match_end`) and advances. The disconnect goes through the death path (UC-030 step 9, UC-020 A2).
2. A waiting player, or the winner of a match decided in the same frame, is out of the bracket (`alive` false, eliminated, through the same death path). The other player of its next match advances without playing (a walkover: no `match_start` and no `match_end`), at once when that player is known, otherwise when it wins its own match.
3. Use case continues at step 4 (D-G33).

## Postconditions

### Success Postconditions

- The champion or leader is in the event stream; the session is ENDED.

### Failure Postconditions

- `game_error` ends the session.

## Business Rules

### BR-001: Invincibility composes with agent shields

A player cannot die while `now < max(agent shield, match invincibility)` (#817).

### BR-002: Fixed tempo

Both modes play without tempo changes (D-G3; moved from UC-031 BR-001 by #1500): no tempo loop by design, the music plays at 1.0 and the slow threshold table applies all game. Both run the base game loop and the EMA warm-up (Tournament since #1882). Fight Club and Tournament detect hits for their two fighters with UC-030 steps 4–7 and A1 (Tournament: the two fighters of the current match): the `perception.ema_weight` EMA, the EMA reset during grace and invincibility, and at most one warning per 0.5 s window. Waiting players are not checked (no warning, no death), but their name, battery and controller health are still captured (D-G9, #1347; Tournament #1737).

Fight Club and Tournament start like the base `run()`: they set the game's flag evaluation context before any task starts, run the EMA warm-up of the startup guard (UC-030 BR-003) after the countdown and before the first round or match, and end through the shared run-end cleanup (shield feedback ended before the winner effects on the teardown path; the gameplay stream closed, shield charge spans closed and the session analytics cleared on every exit; D-G29, #1938). Tournament also gets the 0.3 s start grace of the base game loop, on top of the per-match invincibility. An error in Tournament's bracket step ends the game with `game_error` (no `completed` outcome) instead of a stream reconnect. Tests: `services/game_coordinator/tests/test_fight_club_run.py`, `services/game_coordinator/tests/test_tournament_run.py`.

Known gap (end of game): a force end still declares a winner next to `game_force_ended`: the end-game step publishes the winner, the rainbow, the win voice and `game_ended` (issue #1343, D-G16; moved from UC-027 BR-001 by #1602).

Known gap (step 3, A2): the base death accounting runs before the mode can decline a kill, for a waiting Fight Club player, a Tournament player between matches (since #1882 only through an `eliminate_player` intervention: its motion is no longer checked), the winner of a decided match or a fighter in grace: the player's `game_player_alive` gauge is set to 0 (and stays 0), a second `SFX_EXPLOSION` plays, `dead_count` and the elimination order are bumped and a `player_death` span event is added (issue #1383).

Known gap (preconditions): `lib/types.py` declares a minimum of 3 players for Tournament, but only the two-player minimum of UC-010 BR-001 is enforced (D-G2, issue #1862).

Known gap (step 5, A3): the tournament loop stops when one player is left without running the win check, so neither `tournament_champion` nor `tournament_no_winner` is published, the champion gets no rainbow or voice, and `game_ended` carries no champion (issue #1337).

### BR-003: Root-span outcome

Fight Club and Tournament run their own `run()` and record the game root-span outcome as in UC-020 BR-003: `completed` for a natural end, or `force_ended` with the force-end reason (`game.end_reason`) when force-ended. A force-end after the loop ended does not rewrite the outcome.

### BR-004: One loser per match

In Tournament, the first kill of a match decides it. A second lethal sample in the same frame (a double hit), once the match is decided, does not eliminate the winner: the winner stays in the bracket and advances (step 4), and only the loser gets `GAME_EFFECT_PLAYER_DEATH`. A kill the mode ignores (a waiting player, a player in grace, a decided match) gets no death effect (D-G30, #1991).

The base death accounting that runs before the handler declines such a kill is the step 3 Known gap under BR-002 (issue #1383).
