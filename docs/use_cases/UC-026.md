# Use Case: Play Non-Stop Joust

## Overview

**Use Case ID:** UC-026
**Use Case Name:** Play Non-Stop Joust
**Primary Actor:** Player
**Goal:** Die least often before the time limit
**Status:** Tested
**Requirements:** FR-026, FR-033

## Preconditions

- A NonStop game was started with at least two players (UC-010).

## Main Success Scenario

1. System starts the game; every player begins with a score of 100.
2. A player whose movement exceeds the threshold dies (`player_death`), loses 10 points and respawns after `nonstop.respawn_seconds` (default 3 s) with `nonstop.spawn_protection_seconds` (default 2 s) of protection (`player_respawned`).
3. When `nonstop.time_limit_seconds` is reached, System picks the highest score, publishes `game_winner` and `game_ended`.

## Alternative Flows

### A1: Unlimited time

**Trigger:** `nonstop.time_limit_seconds` is 0 (default)
**Flow:**

1. The game never ends by itself; an operator ends it (UC-027).

## Postconditions

### Success Postconditions

- The highest scorer is in the event stream; the session is ENDED.

### Failure Postconditions

- `game_error` ends the session.

## Business Rules

### BR-001: Scoring

Score = max(0, 100 − 10 × deaths), configurable through `nonstop.scoring`.

**Code / tests:** `services/game_coordinator/games/nonstop_joust.py`; tests `tests/integration/test_parallel_lifecycle.py` (NonStop).
