# Use Case: Play Team Joust

## Overview

**Use Case ID:** UC-021
**Use Case Name:** Play Team Joust (Teams, Random Teams)
**Primary Actor:** Player
**Goal:** Be on the last team with players alive
**Status:** Tested
**Requirements:** FR-021, FR-027, FR-030

## Preconditions

- A JoustTeams or JoustRandomTeams game was started with at least two players (UC-010). `num_teams` (2 to 6, default 2) comes from `game.json`.

## Main Success Scenario

1. System sorts players by serial, shuffles them if `random_assignment` is on, and assigns teams round-robin.
2. System colours each player with the team colour and runs the team countdown (team colour, white, green).
3. Players play as in UC-020 steps 3 to 5.
4. When only one team has players alive, System publishes `team_winner` (team, name, colour, winners), shows the winners a rainbow and announces "<colour> team wins".
5. System publishes `game_ended`.

## Alternative Flows

### A1: Random Teams formation

**Trigger:** Mode is JoustRandomTeams (step 1)
**Flow:**

1. System shuffles team colours and assigns players from a shuffled pool (team sizes are not guaranteed equal).
2. System runs a 5 s `team_formation` phase (`team_formation_start` / `team_formation_end`) so players find their team.
3. Use case continues at step 2.

### A2: All teams eliminated together

**Trigger:** No team has players alive (step 4)
**Flow:**

1. System publishes `game_tie`.
2. Use case continues at step 5.

## Postconditions

### Success Postconditions

- The winning team is in the event stream; the session is ENDED.

### Failure Postconditions

- `game_error` ends the session.

## Business Rules

### BR-001: Team colours

The first N of 8 team colours are used.
