# Use Case: Play Team Joust

## Overview

**Use Case ID:** UC-021
**Use Case Name:** Play Team Joust (Teams, Random Teams)
**Primary Actor:** Player
**Goal:** Be on the last team with players alive
**Status:** Tested
**Requirements:** FR-021, FR-143, FR-146

## Preconditions

- A JoustTeams or JoustRandomTeams game was started with at least three players, the mode's minimum (UC-010 BR-001). `num_teams` (2 to 6, default 2) comes from `game.json`.

## Main Success Scenario

1. System sorts players by serial, shuffles them if `random_assignment` is on, and assigns teams round-robin.
2. System colours each player with the team colour and runs the team countdown (team colour, white, green).
3. Players play as in UC-020 steps 3 to 5; deaths are detected as in [UC-030](UC-030.md) (Teams uses the default kill gate).
4. When only one team has players alive, System publishes `team_winner` (team, name, colour, winners), shows the winners a rainbow and announces "<colour> team wins" (Blue, Magenta, Green, Yellow and Turquoise have a voice line; Pink, Orange and Purple fall back to "congratulations").
5. System publishes `game_ended` with `winning_team` (the team index, `none` when no team won), `outcome` and, after a force-end, `reason` (BR-003).

## Alternative Flows

### A1: Random Teams formation

**Trigger:** Mode is JoustRandomTeams (step 1)
**Flow:**

1. System shuffles the team colours, shuffles the players (sorted by serial) and assigns them round-robin; team sizes differ by at most one; with fewer players than teams the extra teams stay empty.
2. System opens the gameplay stream, then runs a 5 s `team_formation` phase (`team_formation_start` / `team_formation_end`) so players find their team: each controller pulses its team colour over the gameplay stream, then keeps it as its base colour. Frames that arrive during the formation and the instructions clip are discarded. A disconnect seen in that time is handled when the game loop starts (UC-020 A2), unless the controller's frames came back before the formation and the instructions ended (UC-002 BR-004); a drop and reconnect during the countdown is still eliminated. A stream that breaks or stalls in that time is opened once more when the instructions end.
3. Use case continues at step 2.

### A2: All teams eliminated together

**Trigger:** No team has players alive (step 4)
**Flow:**

1. System publishes `game_tie`.
2. Use case continues at step 5.

## Postconditions

### Success Postconditions

- The winning team is in the event stream; the session is ENDED.

### Failure Postconditions

- `game_error` ends the session.

## Business Rules

### BR-001: Team colours

Teams uses the first N of 8 team colours; Random Teams uses a random N of the 8.

### BR-002: Root-span outcome

The team modes use the base `run()` and record the game root-span outcome (`game.outcome`, telemetry.md §3.2) as in UC-020 BR-003: `completed` for a natural end, or `force_ended` with the force-end reason (`game.end_reason`) when force-ended. A force-end after the loop ended does not rewrite the outcome.

### BR-003: Force-end declares no winner; the win is latched

A force-end declares no winner (issue #2003, as in UC-027 step 3): while the game runs, no `team_winner`, no winner rainbow, no team victory voice and no rainbow wait happen, the surviving player and team spans end with `winner=false`, and `game_ended` carries `winning_team` `none` with `outcome` `force_ended` and `reason`. The result (the winning team, or the tie of A2) is latched once, in the same step that decides it and before `team_winner` or `game_tie` is published, together with the root-span outcome (BR-002): a force-end that lands afterwards (the same tick, the publish, the hold or the rainbow) keeps the winner, its end-of-game effects and `outcome` `completed`, and a later win check does not publish it again. The root-span outcome is latched first: a force-end that lands before it (also after the win check started) latches `force_ended` and no result, so nothing is published. A gameplay stream that ends before a result also ends with `winning_team` `none`, `outcome` `completed` and no winner effects.
