# Use Case: Play a Hidden-Role Game

## Overview

**Use Case ID:** UC-022
**Use Case Name:** Play a Hidden-Role Game (Traitor, Werewolf)
**Primary Actor:** Player
**Goal:** Win for your secret side
**Status:** Tested
**Requirements:** FR-022, FR-147

## Preconditions

- A Traitor or Werewolf game was started with at least the mode's minimum players: four for Traitor, three for Werewolf (UC-010 BR-001).

## Main Success Scenario

1. For Traitor, System builds teams (the `num_teams` flag, default 2, as the menu sends it; only when no team count is given: 2 teams below 9 players, otherwise up to 3) and picks traitors per the `traitor.count_tiers` flag (≤ 5 players: 1, ≤ 8: 2, ≤ 11: 3, else n / 3), capped at ⌊(n − 1) / 2⌋ so loyal players outnumber traitors: at least 1 from 3 players, and a 2-player game has no traitor.
2. System gives each traitor a secret team different from its visible team, opens the gameplay stream and runs a 5 s `traitor_signal` phase (`traitor_signal_start` / `traitor_signal_end`): each controller pulses its visible team colour and then keeps it as its base colour, and each traitor feels a rumble (BR-003).
3. Players play; deaths use the normal thresholds (UC-030).
4. When only one secret team has players alive, System publishes `traitor_winner` (team, winners, traitor and loyal winners), shows the winners a rainbow with the traitor win voice, and publishes `game_ended` with `winning_team` (the secret team index, `none` when no team won), `traitor_count`, `outcome` and, after a force-end, `reason` (BR-005).

## Alternative Flows

### A1: Werewolf

**Trigger:** Mode is Werewolf (step 1)
**Flow:**

1. System picks `max(1, int(n × werewolf.werewolf_fraction))` werewolves (default fraction 0.44); everyone is yellow.
2. System opens the gameplay stream and runs a `werewolf_intro` over it: all controllers yellow and a rumble to the werewolves only; frames that arrive during the intro are read and discarded (only disconnects are kept, as in UC-021 A1). After the countdown System primes the EMA (the startup guard, UC-030 BR-003) and reveals the werewolves (light blue) `werewolf.reveal_time_seconds` (default 35 s) after gameplay starts, with `werewolf_reveal`. A reveal timer that fires once a side has won (the win hold) or after the game stopped reveals nothing and publishes no `werewolf_reveal` (BR-006). A reveal LED write that fails on a gameplay stream that has died is skipped: the other werewolves are still revealed, and the reopened stream restores the light blue (UC-030 BR-006, #2022). Event order: `game_starting` → `players_initialized` → `werewolf_intro_start` / `werewolf_intro_end` → `countdown_start` / `countdown_end` → `game_started` → `werewolf_reveal` (Werewolf publishes `game_started` after the countdown, the base modes before it).
3. Werewolves use fixed thresholds from `werewolf.thresholds` that ignore tempo and agent factors; humans use the normal slow thresholds (BR-001).
4. When all humans or all werewolves are dead, System publishes `werewolf_winner` and `game_ended`.
5. Use case ends.

### A2: Tie

**Trigger:** No secret team survives (step 4), or no human and no werewolf survives (A1 step 4)
**Flow:**

1. System publishes `game_tie`; Werewolf publishes no `werewolf_winner` in this case.
2. Use case ends.

### A3: Instructions on

**Trigger:** The host has switched spoken instructions on (`game_instructions`, FR-014, UC-012 A3) when the game starts (step 2, A1 step 2)
**Flow:**

1. System plays the mode's clip as in UC-020 A5 and waits for it before the music and the countdown: Traitor plays `Traitor-instructions` after the `traitor_signal` phase, Werewolf plays `werewolf-instructions` after the `werewolf_intro`. The gameplay stream is open and drained meanwhile, as during the phase (frames discarded, disconnects kept). A force-end during the wait skips the countdown and the start, as in UC-020 A5 step 2.
2. Use case continues at step 3 (Werewolf: A1 step 3).

## Postconditions

### Success Postconditions

- The winning side (`traitor_winner` or `werewolf_winner`) or `game_tie` (A2) is in the event stream; the session is ENDED.

### Failure Postconditions

- `game_error` ends the session.

## Business Rules

### BR-001: Fixed tempo in Werewolf

By design (maintainer decision 2026-10-22, #1273), Werewolf runs without the tempo music loop: music stays at normal speed (1.0) and the slow threshold table applies all game. Werewolf runs its own `run()` around the base game loop; like the base start it publishes `game_starting`, refuses fewer than two players with `ValueError` before the stream opens (the session publishes `game_error`, UC-010 BR-001), runs the EMA warm-up of the startup guard (UC-030 BR-003) and sets the game's flag evaluation context (D-G3; moved from UC-031 BR-001 by #1500).

Flag context: the session already sets `game_kind` and the experiment (`experiment_id`, `arm`) before the game is created (`game_session.py`), so a shadow Werewolf game resolved experiments before #1273 too. The Werewolf start adds `targetingKey=game_mode`, `game_mode`, `controller_count` and `sensitivity` for the flag reads made during the game (today the shield activation mode and charge delay); it adds no experiment exposure.

### BR-002: Agent restrictions

The coordinator's mode capability matrix denies controller effects, revive and global sensitivity / difficulty interventions in Werewolf and Traitor.

Tempo and pacing interventions are accepted in Werewolf but have no effect, because Werewolf has no tempo loop (BR-001); denying them in the matrix is a product follow-up.

### BR-003: Team effects over the stream

Traitor opens the gameplay stream before the `traitor_signal` phase, so the team pulse, the persistent team colour and the traitor rumble go over it; the stream opens with every controller white. Frames that arrive during the phase and the instructions clip are discarded, with the same disconnect and reopen handling as Random Teams ([UC-021](UC-021.md) A1 step 2). The rumble goes only to the traitors. (D-G10 resolved by #1738.)

### BR-004: Root-span outcome

Werewolf runs its own `run()` and records the game root-span outcome as in UC-020 BR-003: `completed` for a natural end, or `force_ended` with the force-end reason (`game.end_reason`) when force-ended. A force-end after the result was decided (BR-004 below) or after the loop ended does not rewrite the outcome.

A force-end declares no winner (issues #1343, #2009, as in UC-027 step 3): while the game runs, no `werewolf_winner`, no `game_tie`, no winner rainbow, no win voice and no rainbow wait happen, the player spans end with `winner=false` and `winning_team` `none`, and `game_ended` carries `winner` `none` with `outcome` `force_ended` and `reason`. The win check returns no result once the game is force-ended. The result (humans, werewolves, or the tie of A2) is latched once, in the same step that decides it and before `werewolf_winner` or `game_tie` is published, together with the root-span outcome: a force-end that lands afterwards (the same tick, the publish, the hold or the rainbow) keeps the result, its end-of-game effects and `outcome` `completed`, and a later win check does not publish it again (before #2009 the outcome was latched only when the loop ended, so a force-end during the `werewolf_winner` publish reported `force_ended` with the winner kept). The root-span outcome is latched first: a force-end that lands before it (also from the servicer thread, after the win check started) latches `force_ended` and no result, so nothing is published. A gameplay stream that ends without a result also ends with `winner` `none`, `outcome` `completed` and no winner effects.

### BR-005: Traitor force-end declares no winner; the win is latched

A Traitor force-end declares no winner (issue #2003, as in UC-027 step 3): while the game runs, no `traitor_winner`, no winner rainbow, no traitor win voice and no rainbow wait happen, every surviving player span ends with `winner=false`, and `game_ended` carries `winning_team` `none` (before #2003: `-1` for no winner) with `outcome` `force_ended` and `reason`. The result (the winning secret team, or the tie of A2) is latched once, in the same step that decides it and before `traitor_winner` or `game_tie` is published, together with the root-span outcome: a force-end that lands afterwards (the same tick, the publish, the hold or the rainbow) keeps the winner, its end-of-game effects and `outcome` `completed` (as Werewolf, BR-004), and a later win check does not publish it again. The root-span outcome is latched first: a force-end that lands before it (also from the servicer thread, after the win check started) latches `force_ended` and no result, so nothing is published. A gameplay stream that ends before a result also ends with `winning_team` `none`, `outcome` `completed` and no winner effects. Traitor uses the base `run()`, so its root-span outcome follows UC-020 BR-003 as in BR-004.

### BR-006: No Werewolf reveal after the result

Once the result is latched (BR-004) or the game has stopped running, the werewolf reveal does not go on (issue #2022): a reveal timer that fires then reveals nothing and publishes no `werewolf_reveal`, and a result latched during the reveal (the `werewolf_reveal` publish, the reveal voice, or the flash of an earlier werewolf) stops it before the next werewolf. Only the werewolves actually revealed turn light blue, are marked revealed and get the `werewolf_revealed` span event.
