# Use Case: Play Zombies

## Overview

**Use Case ID:** UC-023
**Use Case Name:** Play Zombies
**Primary Actor:** Player
**Goal:** Survive as a human until time runs out, or (as a zombie) convert every human
**Status:** Implemented
**Requirements:** FR-023, FR-142

## Preconditions

- A Zombies game was started with at least two players (UC-010).

## Main Success Scenario

1. System makes `zombie.initial_count` players (default 2, at most players − 1) zombies after a `zombie_intro`.
2. System starts a timer of `((players × 3) / 16) × 60` seconds and announces remaining time (1 min, 30 s, 10 s).
3. A human whose movement exceeds the threshold is converted to a zombie (`human_converted`) and gets a grace period of `death_grace_period_seconds`.
4. A zombie whose movement exceeds its fixed threshold (`zombie.thresholds`) dies and respawns after a random time between `zombie.respawn_min_seconds` and `zombie.respawn_max_seconds` (default 2 to 10 s).
5. When the timer expires with humans alive, System publishes `zombie_winner` (humans) and `game_ended`.

## Alternative Flows

### A1: All humans converted

**Trigger:** No humans left (step 3)
**Flow:**

1. System publishes `zombie_winner` (zombies) and `game_ended`.
2. Use case ends.

## Postconditions

### Success Postconditions

- The winning side is in the event stream; the session is ENDED.

### Failure Postconditions

- `game_error` ends the session.

## Business Rules

### BR-001: Duration formula

The docstring claims ~3 minutes for 4 players, but the formula gives 45 s for 4 and 135 s for 12 players (D-G6).

### BR-002: Fixed tempo

Zombies runs its own game loop without tempo changes (D-G3; moved from UC-031 BR-001 by #1500). Zombies use the fixed thresholds of `zombie.thresholds`, ignoring tempo and agent factors (moved from UC-030 A3 by #1500).

Known gap (steps 3, 4): Zombies skips the EMA priming of the startup guard (UC-030 BR-003) (issue #1273, D-G3; moved from UC-030 BR-003 by #1500).

Known gap (preconditions): `lib/types.py` declares a minimum of 4 players for Zombies, but only the two-player minimum of UC-010 BR-001 is enforced (D-G2, issue #1277).

Known gap (steps 3, 4): the base death effect runs after the conversion or respawn, so a converted human or a respawned zombie keeps a black LED while still playing (issue #1342).
